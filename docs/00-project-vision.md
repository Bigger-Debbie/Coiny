# Project Vision

**Project:** Coiny
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Vision Statement

Coiny is a modern household finance platform designed to help individuals and families better understand, organize, and plan their financial future.

Rather than functioning solely as an expense tracker, Coiny aims to become a complete financial management system that enables users to confidently answer important financial questions, make informed decisions, and build healthy financial habits.

The long-term vision is to provide a single place where users can monitor their finances, create budgets, track progress toward goals, forecast future scenarios, and gain meaningful insight into their financial health.

---

# Purpose

Personal finance is often spread across multiple bank accounts, credit cards, investment accounts, budgeting tools, and spreadsheets.

Coiny exists to consolidate this information into a single, intuitive platform that provides both visibility into current finances and confidence when planning for the future.

The goal is not simply to record financial data, but to transform that data into actionable insights.

---

# Problem Statement

Managing personal finances can be overwhelming.

Many existing finance applications focus on recording transactions but provide limited assistance when users need to answer questions such as:

* Where is our money going?
* Are we staying within our monthly budget?
* Can we afford a large purchase?
* How much should we save each month?
* How is our financial position changing over time?
* Are we making progress toward our financial goals?

Coiny seeks to answer these questions through thoughtful design, clear reporting, and intelligent planning tools.

---

# Target Audience

Coiny is designed for:

* Individuals managing their personal finances
* Couples managing shared household finances
* Families planning for long-term financial goals

The initial implementation is being designed around a shared household model that allows multiple members to participate in managing a common financial picture.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Design Before Implementation

Every feature should be understood, documented, and designed before development begins.

Thoughtful architecture reduces technical debt and creates software that is easier to maintain and extend.

---

## Financial Accuracy

Financial data must be trustworthy.

Transactions serve as the primary source of truth, and calculated values should be derived from the transaction history whenever practical.

---

## Simplicity

Coiny should make complex financial information easier to understand without sacrificing accuracy.

The user experience should prioritize clarity over unnecessary complexity.

---

## Incremental Development

Coiny will be built feature by feature, with each milestone producing a stable and functional application.

Every completed feature should improve the usefulness of the platform.

---

## Maintainability

Code should be written for the next developer—even if that developer is your future self.

Readability, consistency, and documentation are valued over clever implementations.

# Core Product Pillars

## 1. Understand

Provide a clear view of current financial health.

Examples:

* Account balances
* Spending history
* Income tracking
* Net worth

---

## 2. Organize

Keep financial information structured and easy to manage.

Examples:

* Accounts
* Categories
* Financial institutions
* Transactions

---

## 3. Plan

Help users prepare for future financial decisions.

Examples:

* Monthly budgets
* Savings goals
* Planned purchases
* Debt payoff planning

---

## 4. Analyze

Turn financial data into meaningful insights.

Examples:

* Spending trends
* Budget performance
* Cash flow
* Historical comparisons

---

## 5. Improve

Help users build healthier financial habits through visibility, planning, and informed decision-making.

---

# Questions Coiny Should Answer

Every feature developed for Coiny should help answer one or more meaningful financial questions.

Examples include:

* How much money do we currently have?
* Where is our money going?
* Are we staying within budget?
* Can we afford this purchase?
* How much should we save each month?
* What bills are coming due?
* How is our financial position changing over time?
* Are we making progress toward our goals?

If a proposed feature does not help answer a meaningful financial question or improve the user's understanding of their finances, it should be carefully evaluated before implementation.

# Long-Term Vision

Coiny is intended to evolve into a complete household financial platform.

Potential future capabilities include:

* Shared household management
* Budgeting
* Financial goals
* Net worth tracking
* Investment tracking
* Recurring transactions
* Subscription management
* Bill reminders
* Financial forecasting
* Future purchase planning
* Interactive dashboards
* Data import from financial institutions
* Reporting and analytics

---

# Definition of Success

Coiny will be considered successful when it becomes the primary financial dashboard used by its users to:

* Monitor day-to-day finances.
* Manage household budgets.
* Plan future purchases.
* Track financial goals.
* Understand spending habits.
* Make informed financial decisions with confidence.

---

# Vision Statement Summary

Coiny is more than an expense tracker.

Its purpose is to help people understand their finances, plan for the future, and make better financial decisions through thoughtful software design, accurate financial modeling, and an intuitive user experience.
