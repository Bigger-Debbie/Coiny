How this estimate works

Actuals use posted income, expenses, and assigned contributions. Transfers between household accounts do not create income. Recorded income offsets expected income within each pay period.

Future periods use the published budget, including dated updates. Remaining allowances and unpaid commitments are estimates; link payments and assign expenses to keep them accurate. Ranges finish at a pay-period boundary, so the final date may extend past the selected number of months.
